MilkMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

MilkMate is a dairy management application that helps dairy businesses manage customers, milk deliveries, suppliers, staff, expenses, payments, inventory and reports. This Privacy Policy explains what information MilkMate collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Important: Before publishing this page, replace support.milkmate@gmail.com with the real support/contact email address used for MilkMate.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MilkMate may process:

  • Account information: mobile/phone number and authentication information used to create and sign in to an account.
  • Business information: business/account information and account start date.
  • Customer and supplier information: names or business names, phone numbers, areas, milk type, pricing and delivery-related details entered by the account owner.
  • Staff information: staff login identifiers, permissions, assigned customers/routes and related account information entered by the business owner.
  • Operational records: milk delivery/collection quantities, orders, payments, expenses, inventory and related reports entered by users.
  • Device/local data: information that MilkMate stores locally on the device to support app operation, preferences and backup-related features.

2. How We Use Information

MilkMate uses information to:

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • provide dairy management features;
  • store and synchronize business data;
  • manage staff access and permissions;
  • calculate and display operational reports;
  • provide backup and restore functionality where available;
  • maintain security and prevent unauthorized access; and
  • respond to support requests.

3. Cloud Services

MilkMate uses Google Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage. Firebase Authentication is used for account authentication, and Cloud Firestore is used to store business data associated with MilkMate accounts.

Firebase processes information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. MilkMate uses account/business identifiers and access controls so that business data is associated with the appropriate account.

4. Sharing of Information

MilkMate does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate MilkMate, including Firebase services used for authentication and cloud storage. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and rights of MilkMate and its users.

5. Staff and Business Users

MilkMate allows a business owner to create staff accounts and assign permissions. Business owners are responsible for entering accurate information and for using the staff-management and permission features appropriately.

6. Data Security

MilkMate uses authentication and access controls intended to protect account and business information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their login credentials and devices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

MilkMate retains account and business information for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain the account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Users may request deletion of their MilkMate account and associated personal information by contacting support.milkmate@gmail.com. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security or accounting purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

MilkMate is intended for dairy businesses and general business users. It is not directed to children under 13, and MilkMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

MilkMate may use third-party services required to operate the application. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MilkMate's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
